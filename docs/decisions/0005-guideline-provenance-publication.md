Alternative: retain private visibility pending clarification; that factual gap
is now resolved for the owned guideline. Removing or rewriting text would not
erase provenance or make previous Git history disappear. History is preserved.

whitepaper and artwork bytes and branding exclusion. Audit tracked history before

specific third-party fragment or other provenance evidence is identified.
